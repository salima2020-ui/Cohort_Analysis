##1. Customer Retention Insights
Sharp Initial Drop-off Rate
The vast majority of customers do not return for a repeat purchase in period 1 after their initial order (period 0). The retention rate drops sharply below 2% in the very first month.

Retention Plateau
From period 2 onwards, the small percentage of returning customers remains steady, indicating a small but highly loyal core customer base.

##2. Revenue & LTV (Cumulative Revenue) Insights
Revenue Concentration in Period 0
Over 90% of total customer lifetime value (LTV) is generated during the first month of acquisition. Repeat purchase revenue contributes a minimal percentage to overall revenue.

Seasonality Effects
Cohorts acquired in Q4 (specifically November and December) generate significantly higher initial revenue (period 0) due to holiday shopping, but follow the same steep drop-off in subsequent periods.

3. Purchase Depth & Logistics (Items & Freight per User)
Basket Size (Items per User)
Average cumulative items per user start between 1.1 - 1.2 units in period 0 and plateau shortly after, pointing to weak cross-selling efficiency post-acquisition.

Shipping Cost Friction (Freight per User)
Cumulative freight cost grows marginally after period 0. High shipping costs relative to product price serve as a primary friction point discouraging repeat orders.

4. Regional & Geographic Insights
Geographic Concentration
Customer acquisition and repeat orders are heavily concentrated in primary hubs like SP (São Paulo) and RJ (Rio de Janeiro).

Logistics Impact
Outer regional states show noticeably lower retention rates, driven by higher delivery fees and longer fulfillment timelines.

5. Strategic Recommendations & Actionable Next Steps
Automated Post-Purchase Onboarding (Period 1 Retention)
Trigger targeted email/push campaigns with tailored discounts 15–20 days after the initial purchase to incentivize second-time orders.

Loyalty & Subscription Incentives (Free Shipping / Cashback)
Introduce free shipping thresholds or cashback incentives for returning buyers to offset freight friction and improve repeat order frequency.

Cross-Selling & Product Bundling
Enhance "frequently bought together" recommendation engines on product pages to drive higher basket sizes during the initial purchase (period 0).
